# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 2 (Revenue Expansion). FinWise has achieved core product-market fit at $10M ARR, but the growth engine has stalled due to structural packaging failures.

**Model:** Subscription via Reverse-Trial. We use the 14-day reverse trial to get founders to connect their data and hit the "Aha!" moment of runway forecasting, but transition them to a strict subscription to unlock external utility.

_____

## Recommendation

We are shifting to a value-gated packaging model to solve both churn and conversion simultaneously.

**What is free:** We allow free on-screen modeling and access to the "Capital Efficiency Widget" to drive daily habituation (fixing the 60% churn problem).

**Where the paywall sits:** We place a hard Pro paywall directly in front of the Export/Share feature. Users must upgrade to download, export, or share their financial models with investors, accountants, or lenders. We monetize the external utility, not the internal tool.

_____

## Model signal

(What success looks like in the data). Success looks like a complete shift in when users decide to pull out their credit cards. Instead of random, trickling upgrades throughout the 14-day trial, conversions will cluster tightly at the paywall boundary—specifically, the exact moment a founder tries to export their generated PDF to send to a stakeholder. This signal proves we have successfully aligned our monetization trigger with their moment of maximum external value.
