# The Signals · FinWise

> Module 4 · Data & Analytics. The data pattern you found and the indicators you'll steer by.

## The data pattern

Our 13-month funnel analysis revealed a completely broken relationship between product engagement and revenue. Usage of the core "Financial Modeling" feature skyrocketed 5x, yet the trial-to-paid conversion rate remained completely flat at a 2% ceiling. The real problem is a packaging and habituation failure: the free tier gives away the entire predictive value of the product without requiring payment, and it lacks a daily operational hook to keep users logging in, leading to the massive churn.

## Leading indicators

**Widget Execution Velocity:** The frequency and volume of "Actionable Inefficiencies" cleared per user session.

**Feature Usage % (Financial Modeling):** The rate at which users actively run new "What-If" scenarios.

**Why these matter:** These are highly movable metrics that prove daily habituation early in the trial window. Waiting to measure a 2% lagging conversion rate takes too long to reach statistical significance in an A/B test.

## Lagging indicators

**Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate (Currently 2%):** The ultimate measure of our monetization fix (the gated PDF export).

**1-Year Paid Retention Rate (Currently 40% / 60% Churn):** The long-term measure of our engagement fix (the daily efficiency widget).
